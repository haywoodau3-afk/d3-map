# Third-party and backend policy

d3map is BSD-3-Clause licensed. Runtime Python dependencies are NumPy, Matplotlib, openpyxl, and ReportLab; PySide6 is used by the optional desktop application. pytest, mypy, Ruff, PyInstaller, and platform installer tools are development or packaging dependencies and retain their own licences.

xTB and CREST are separately installed external programs with their own licences and citation requirements. They are not bundled, downloaded, or redistributed by d3map. External DFT programs and user-provided results remain subject to their respective licences. `d3map doctor` reports discoverable executables; published analyses should retain the backend versions, commands, methods, radii profiles, and population provenance written by the workflow.
